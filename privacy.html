Manhtv Phim Hay Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Manhtv Phim Hay Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

2. Data we access

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) – scope user.info.basic;
  • permission to upload and publish videos – scopes video.upload and video.publish.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account and to show the upload status to the owner.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

5. Other users

The app does not collect any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

6. Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version is always available at this link.

8. Contact

For questions, contact the channel owner via the TikTok account linked to this app.
